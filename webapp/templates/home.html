How the system works

The end-to-end NLP workflow transforming unstructured student comments into facility metrics.

1

Comments intake

800 raw student feedback comments collected across eight campus facilities.

2

Topic extraction

Hybrid NLP identifies specific facility aspects such as seating, noise, or price.

3

Sentiment scoring

DistilBERT classifies each extracted aspect as positive, neutral, or negative.

4

Aggregation

Calculates 1 to 5 facility ratings and detects recurring negative problem areas.

5

Summaries

Hand-corrected executive summaries highlight key findings for administration.

6

Toxicity filtering

Toxic-BERT isolates abusive submissions so facility ratings remain unbiased.
